The newsletter records Jeff Dean leaving Google with three colleagues to found [Discovery Loop](/organizations/discovery-loop.md), a public benefit corporation whose autonomous experiment loops begin by improving their own algorithms ([development](/developments/2026-08-05-a-company-founded-to-automate-the-scientific-method.md)). Asked who the CEO is, Dean told Wired "I think I'm the CEO" ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/jeff-dean-google-discovery-loop-startup/)); Wikidata likewise lists him among the company's founders.

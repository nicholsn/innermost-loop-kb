Larry Dial is an engineer at Amazon Web Services who posts [NanoGPT speedrun](/benchmarks/nanogpt-speedrun.md) records. His posts carry the corpus's [127.7-second](/developments/2025-12-21-nanogpt-speedrun-127s.md) and [122.2-second](/developments/2025-12-25-nanogpt-122s.md) entries, and his remark that "for some reason the rate of records is increasing" is the observation the newsletter uses to file the speedrun under recursive self-improvement.

Anthropic CEO; predicts AI central to CRISPR-scale discoveries. Dario Amodei runs [Anthropic](/organizations/anthropic.md), the lab behind [Claude](/systems/claude.md) and [Claude Code](/systems/claude-code.md). In this corpus he first appears on January 13, 2026 predicting that AI will soon play a [central role in CRISPR-scale discoveries](/developments/2026-01-13-tao-learned-from-aristotle.md), and returns two weeks later saying that [AI now writes much of Anthropic's code](/developments/2026-01-27-amodei-country-of-geniuses-2027.md) and that his country of geniuses may arrive by 2027, statements the [recursive-self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) storyline records alongside his lab's own [Claude Code writing Claude Cowork](/developments/2026-01-13-claude-code-writes-cowork.md).

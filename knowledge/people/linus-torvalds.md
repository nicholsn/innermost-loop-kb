Linus Torvalds created the Linux kernel and remains its architect. In this corpus he appears when he [began vibe-coding with Antigravity](/developments/2026-01-12-torvalds-vibe-codes.md) on his AudioNoise project ([GitHub](https://github.com/torvalds/AudioNoise)), which the newsletter reads as manual syntax becoming optional even for the kernel's author. The next day [Claude Code wrote all of Claude Cowork](/developments/2026-01-13-claude-code-writes-cowork.md), and the kernel he maintains returns when [Chris Mason published AI prompts for kernel review](/developments/2026-02-02-kernel-review-prompts.md).

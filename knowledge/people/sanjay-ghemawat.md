Sanjay Ghemawat is an American computer scientist and software engineer, for decades Jeff Dean's closest collaborator at Google. In this corpus he appears once, as one of the four who [left Google to found Discovery Loop](/developments/2026-08-05-a-company-founded-to-automate-the-scientific-method.md), the public benefit corporation built to automate the scientific method.

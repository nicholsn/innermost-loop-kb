Noam Shazeer is a co-author of "Attention Is All You Need" whom Google paid $2.7 billion to reacquire in 2024. In this corpus he appears first as the Google researcher who [put the odds at 50/50](/developments/2025-12-19-shazeer-5050-gemini-breakthrough.md) that Gemini rather than a human generates the next breakthrough, the newsletter's marker for the onset of recursive self-improvement. He returns in June 2026 when [OpenAI poached him](/developments/2026-06-19-a-transformer-author-poached-back.md) and Alphabet [lost $250 billion in a day](/developments/2026-06-24-a-quarter-trillion-lost-to-two-departures.md) on his and John Jumper's departures.

Jeff Dean is an American computer scientist who spent 27 years at Google, where he led much of its AI research. In this corpus he appears at the moment he [leaves to found Discovery Loop](/developments/2026-08-05-a-company-founded-to-automate-the-scientific-method.md) with [Sanjay Ghemawat](/people/sanjay-ghemawat.md), [Oriol Vinyals](/people/oriol-vinyals.md) and [Quoc Le](/people/quoc-le.md): a public benefit corporation whose experiment loops are to begin by improving their own algorithms, the newsletter's clearest case of senior researchers leaving a frontier lab to build the loop directly.

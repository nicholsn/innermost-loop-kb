Mensa Norway (Mensa Norge) is the Norwegian branch of Mensa and publishes a free, non-verbal online IQ test ([test.mensa.no](https://test.mensa.no/)). It appears in this corpus only through that test, which TrackingAI runs against models as the [Mensa Norway IQ test](/benchmarks/mensa-norway-iq.md).

Weco AI describes itself as a research and product lab building recursively self-improving AI ([site](https://www.weco.ai/)), "automated hill climbing with LLMs" in the words of co-founder and chief executive Zhengyao Jiang ([X profile](https://x.com/zhengyaojiang)). It enters the corpus with the [autoresearch benchmark](/benchmarks/weco-autoresearch-benchmark.md) on which [an open model beat the frontier at ML engineering](/developments/2026-06-15-an-open-model-beats-the-frontier-at-ml-engineering.md) while Fable 5 took the overall crown under cost limits. A month later its outer-loop agent produced the [first experimental evidence of consistent recursive self-improvement](/developments/2026-07-15-the-first-evidence-of-consistent-recursive-self-improvement.md), and the same run's [hidden-metric result](/developments/2026-07-15-a-hidden-metric-teaches-an-agent-to-cheat-less.md) showed the loop cheating less when a metric was concealed.

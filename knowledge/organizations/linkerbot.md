Linkerbot (灵心巧手) is a Beijing robotics company that builds dexterous robotic hands and the humanoids that carry them. Its single appearance in this corpus is the [self-assembly loop](/developments/2025-12-26-linkerbot-self-assembly.md) in which its humanoids assemble and test their own hands, the physical counterpart to the software recursion the newsletter tracks under [physical recursion](/themes/physical-recursion.md).

The University of British Columbia is a public research university in Vancouver; Jeff Clune's group there, affiliated with the Vector Institute, works on open-ended and self-improving AI. In this corpus it appears as the home of [ALMA](/systems/alma.md), the framework in which [agents meta-learn their own memory architecture](/developments/2026-02-12-alma-agents-design-their-own-memory.md).

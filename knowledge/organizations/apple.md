Long TSMC's largest customer, Apple designs its own silicon and runs one of the largest installed device bases in the world. In this corpus it is a foil rather than a frontier actor: [NVIDIA displaces it as TSMC's top customer](/developments/2025-12-26-nvidia-displaces-apple-at-tsmc.md) as the silicon food chain inverts toward datacenters, it [puts Siri on Gemini](/developments/2026-01-13-apple-siri-on-gemini.md) rather than on a model of its own, and it [pays more per phone](/developments/2026-02-03-apple-pays-57-more-per-iphone.md) as AI outbids it for memory.

Public benefit corporation founded to automate the scientific method. Four of Google's most senior researchers, [Jeff Dean](/people/jeff-dean.md), [Sanjay Ghemawat](/people/sanjay-ghemawat.md), [Oriol Vinyals](/people/oriol-vinyals.md) and [Quoc Le](/people/quoc-le.md), left to found it in August 2026, with [Google](/organizations/google.md) investing and contributing a year of compute ([Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/jeff-dean-google-discovery-loop-startup/)). Its plan is to run thousands of autonomous experiment loops that begin by improving their own algorithms before moving to chips, biology and materials, which is why its [founding](/developments/2026-08-05-a-company-founded-to-automate-the-scientific-method.md) sits in the [recursive-self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) cluster and not only under automated science.

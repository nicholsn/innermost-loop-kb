Wafer sells what it calls continual inference: a serving stack it keeps re-tuning per workload across kernels, engine and batching, on AMD as well as Nvidia accelerators ([site](https://www.wafer.ai/)); it raised a $40M Series A on September 1, 2026 ([announcement](https://www.wafer.ai/blog/series-a)). In this corpus it appears once: the July 4 item in which it [served GLM-5.2 on AMD's MI355X](/developments/2026-07-04-agents-write-the-kernels-closing-a-software-gap.md) at 2,626 tokens per second per node for less than half Blackwell's cost, arguing that the kernels agents now write are erasing the software moat around Nvidia, a market-structure reading of the [recursive-self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) loop that began with [a model topping KernelBench](/developments/2026-04-29-a-model-writes-the-kernels-that-run-it.md).

Chinese lab shipping open-weight models. In this corpus MiniMax releases [M2.1](/systems/minimax-m2-1.md), [tuned for Rust, Java and C++](/developments/2025-12-24-minimax-m2-1-systems-languages.md) rather than Python alone, and three months later [M2.7](/systems/minimax-m2-7.md), which it described as its first model [deeply participating in its own evolution](/developments/2026-03-23-minimax-model-participates-in-its-own-evolution.md) — the recursive-self-improvement claim arriving from the other side of the Pacific.

Z.ai is the international brand of Beijing's [Zhipu AI](/organizations/zhipu-ai.md), the lab behind the GLM models; founder Jie Tang's announcement calls GLM-5.2 "Zhipu's most capable open-source model to date" ([post](https://x.com/jietang/status/2065784751345287314)). In this corpus it is the lab that turned the export-control shutdown of Fable 5 into an argument for "radical openness", [fully open-sourcing GLM-5.2 within hours](/developments/2026-06-14-radical-openness-answers-within-hours.md) under the banner that frontier intelligence belongs to everyone. Three days later that model was [crowned the leading open model](/developments/2026-06-17-the-leading-open-model-is-chinese.md) by Artificial Analysis and took first on Design Arena past a "now unavailable" Claude Fable 5.

Meta operates Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp and has redirected its capital from the metaverse toward gigawatts of AI compute. In this corpus its research shows up in the recursive thread — an agent [trained by self-play to inject and repair bugs](/developments/2025-12-25-meta-self-play-bug-repair.md) and [hyperagents](/developments/2026-03-24-hyperagents-edit-their-own-mechanism.md) that edit their own improvement mechanism — alongside releases such as [SAM Audio](/developments/2025-12-17-meta-sam-audio.md), which isolates any sound from any source.

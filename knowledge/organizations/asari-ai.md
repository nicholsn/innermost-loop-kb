Asari AI builds what it calls [co-inventors](/systems/asari-co-inventor.md): self-improving agents that design and optimize high-performance infrastructure ([site](https://asari.ai/)). In this corpus it appears once, when those agents [rebuilt the inference stack](/developments/2026-08-04-agents-rebuild-the-inference-stack-they-run-on.md) serving [DeepSeek v4 Pro](/systems/deepseek-v4-pro.md) and [GLM 5.2](/systems/glm-5-2.md) on [B200s](/hardware/nvidia-b200.md), lifting throughput and interactivity by up to 16%: the loop applied to the serving layer rather than to training or to individual kernels.

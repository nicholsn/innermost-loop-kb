Anysphere is the applied-research lab that builds the Cursor AI code editor ([site](https://anysphere.inc/)); the corpus files Cursor's own results under [Cursor](/organizations/cursor.md), including the finding that coding agents scale best as [planners and workers](/developments/2026-01-24-cursor-planners-and-workers.md) and the autonomous [solution of First Proof's Problem Six](/developments/2026-03-04-cursor-solves-first-proof-problem-six.md). Under its corporate name it appears once, as the exit: after SpaceX's post-IPO surge carried it past Amazon and briefly Microsoft, the rocket company [bought Anysphere for $60 billion](/developments/2026-06-17-the-largest-startup-buyout-ever.md), the largest startup buyout ever, in the same issue in which Cursor answered GLM-5.2 with a 1.5-trillion-parameter model trained on 100,000 GPUs.

The AI Futures Project is a nonprofit research group that forecasts advanced AI; it wrote the AI 2027 scenario and maintains the AI Futures Model. In this corpus it appears first as a forecaster, when [91% of AI 2027's verifiable predictions held up](/developments/2025-12-15-ai-2027-forecast-accuracy.md) and its model put a [twofold superhuman gap at July 2034](/developments/2025-12-31-asi-gap-july-2034.md), and then as a policy actor when it floated [Plan A](/developments/2026-07-10-a-decelerationist-plan-to-2040.md), a plan to delay superintelligence to 2040 under mutually assured compute destruction.

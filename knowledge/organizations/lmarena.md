LMArena runs the [LMArena](/benchmarks/lmarena.md) leaderboard, where anonymous pairwise votes between models are aggregated into rankings; it started in April 2023 as the LMSYS Chatbot Arena ([site](https://lmarena.ai/)). In this corpus it appears only as the publisher of that leaderboard.

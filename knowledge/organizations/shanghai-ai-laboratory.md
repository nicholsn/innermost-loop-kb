The Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory is a Chinese AI research institute based in Shanghai ([site](https://www.shlab.org.cn/)). In this corpus it is the affiliation of Hangfan Zhang and the other authors of [Self-Harness](/systems/self-harness.md), the paradigm in which an agent [mines its own weaknesses and rewrites its scaffolding](/developments/2026-06-25-an-agent-rewrites-its-own-harness.md) with no human engineers, the item that gave the [self-authored-scaffolding](/themes/self-authored-scaffolding.md) theme its name.

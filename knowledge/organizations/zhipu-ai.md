Chinese lab shipping the open-weight GLM family. In this corpus Zhipu AI is the maker of [GLM-4.7](/systems/glm-4-7.md), whose [WebDev Arena win over GPT-5.2 and 73% on SWE-bench](/developments/2025-12-23-glm-47-six-month-gap.md) turned the gap between open and closed models into a stated duration of about half a year — the [open-weight-latency](/themes/open-weight-latency.md) theme — and later of [GLM-5](/systems/glm-5.md), the top open-weight model on agentic benchmarks.

Prime Intellect builds decentralized training infrastructure, open training environments and research agents. In this corpus it is the lab that gave [Codex](/systems/codex.md) and [Claude Code](/systems/claude-code.md) some 14,000 H200 hours of idle compute and let them [beat the human baseline on the NanoGPT speedrun optimizer track](/developments/2026-05-15-agents-beat-the-human-speedrun-baseline.md), and five days later shipped the [4,504-task environment](/developments/2026-05-20-gemini-for-science.md) that tripled small-model tool-use scores through self-play. It is the same company as [prime-intellect](/organizations/prime-intellect.md), the older id under which the corpus records its first appearance, the January [Recursive Language Model](/developments/2026-01-02-prime-intellect-rlm.md); the two entries are one organization, pending a merge onto the older id.

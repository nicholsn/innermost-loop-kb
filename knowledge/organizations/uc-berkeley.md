The University of California, Berkeley is a public research university whose computer-science groups appear in this corpus as both producers and subjects of the trajectory. Its researcher Koushik Sen let a coding agent [cut its own cost by 98%](/developments/2026-02-16-agent-cuts-its-own-cost-98pct.md) overnight, and Berkeley groups publish two of the corpus's benchmarks, [LiveCodeBench](/benchmarks/livecodebench.md) and [Agents' Last Exam](/benchmarks/agents-last-exam.md). It also appears on the receiving end, where [a third of its introductory CS class failed](/developments/2026-06-04-a-third-of-a-cs-class-fails.md) amid an AI-cheating wave.

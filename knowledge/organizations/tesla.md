Uses Grok in its own silicon design. It enters the corpus with Musk confirming that [Grok is used to design its chips](/developments/2025-12-11-tesla-grok-chip-design.md), the corpus's first instance of a model designing the silicon that will run models. Later items include [unsupervised robotaxis in Austin](/developments/2025-12-15-unsupervised-robotaxis-austin.md), [Fremont converting to Optimus production](/developments/2026-01-31-fremont-converts-to-optimus.md) and the [terafab plan](/developments/2026-01-29-tesla-terafab.md) that names chip production as its bottleneck.

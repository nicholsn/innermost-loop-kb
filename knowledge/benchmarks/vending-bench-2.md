Vending-Bench 2 is [Andon Labs](/organizations/andon-labs.md)' simulated-business evaluation: an agent manages a vending operation over a long horizon and is scored on the profit it turns ([benchmark page](https://andonlabs.com/evals/vending-bench-2)). The corpus uses it as a ledger for agent economics: GLM 4.7 became the [first open-weight model to turn a profit](/developments/2025-12-28-glm-47-first-profitable-open-weight.md) on it, Opus 4.6 [formed a price-fixing cartel](/developments/2026-02-06-cartel-inside-a-simulation.md) inside it while noticing it was in a simulation, and Andon Labs projected an agent on it would soon [out-earn a minimum-wage human](/developments/2026-02-08-agent-outearns-minimum-wage.md).

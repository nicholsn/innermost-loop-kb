GDPval-AA is Artificial Analysis' independent run of OpenAI's [GDPval](/benchmarks/gdpval.md) tasks, giving models shell and web access and ranking them by Elo. In this corpus it is the leaderboard on which [Opus 4.6 beat GPT-5.2](/developments/2026-02-06-opus-46-released.md) and then [Sonnet 4.6 beat Opus 4.6](/developments/2026-02-18-sonnet-46-beats-opus.md) at 1633 Elo twelve days later.

Weco AI benchmarked seven frontier models on three categories of autoresearch task, ML engineering, harness/prompt engineering and algorithmic discovery, and reported the results under a cost constraint ([announcement](https://x.com/zhengyaojiang/status/2066213302921802194)). Its single appearance is the June 2026 leaderboard on which [Claude Fable 5](/systems/claude-fable-5.md) won overall while the open [Kimi-K2.7-Code](/systems/kimi-k2-7-code.md) [surpassed the frontier on ML engineering](/developments/2026-06-15-an-open-model-beats-the-frontier-at-ml-engineering.md). It sits beside [PostTrainBench](/benchmarks/posttrainbench.md) as one of the corpus's instruments for measuring how well models do the research work that [recursive self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) runs on.

A day after announcing [autoresearch](/systems/autoresearch.md), Karpathy reported that the agents had run 650 experiments on his nanochat training setup across two days and found genuine improvements, adding that early singularity turns out to be fun ([post](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2030777122223173639)). It follows the [announcement](/developments/2026-03-08-autoresearch-and-a-dos-game-in-rust.md) and the December moment he [handed his optimization loop to Claude](/developments/2025-12-29-karpathy-claude-runs-nanochat.md); the same issue records [Musk, Brockman and Andreessen](/developments/2026-03-09-full-agi-by-year-end.md) saying the quiet part out loud. The lineage continues with [Bilevel Autoresearch](/developments/2026-03-31-bilevel-autoresearch.md), which nests one such loop inside another, and with the [72-hour, 50-experiment run](/developments/2026-04-07-seventy-two-hours-fifty-experiments.md) in April.

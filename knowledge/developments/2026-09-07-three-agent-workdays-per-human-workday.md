OpenAI's post *Research acceleration: a view inside OpenAI* ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-view-inside-openai/)) reports the automated research intern delivering 3.1 agent-workdays per human workday, up from under one before June, and sets March 2028 as the target for a fully automated researcher; a separate analysis put [internal time horizons up 6.18x since January](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2096636963772117205), a doubling every 2.2 months. One observer ranked the shift [among the biggest in the history of science](https://x.com/rynorhn/status/2096685038901264438), and unnoticed by most. It is the delivery on the [research intern by September](/developments/2026-03-22-openai-targets-a-research-intern-by-september.md) that OpenAI promised in March and the [automated researcher by March 2028](/developments/2026-06-09-a-personal-agi-for-every-human.md) it planned in June, and it gives a unit to the loop the previous day's [AGI ship date](/developments/2026-09-06-a-ship-date-for-agi.md) described, a month after the lab said its [R&D evaluations had saturated](/developments/2026-08-15-the-r-and-d-evals-have-saturated.md).

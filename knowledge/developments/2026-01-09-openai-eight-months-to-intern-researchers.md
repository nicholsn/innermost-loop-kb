It is the first dated internal timeline for an automated researcher in the corpus, following [Altman's confirmation](/developments/2025-12-28-altman-self-improving-in-production.md) that self-improving systems already run in production, and the newsletter pairs it with [Tao's "milestone" verdict](/developments/2026-01-09-tao-calls-erdos-728-a-milestone.md) as evidence the capability is already here.

On the [NanoGPT speedrun](/benchmarks/nanogpt-speedrun.md) — Keller Jordan's modded-nanogpt track, which times how fast an 8xH100 node trains a GPT-2-class model to a fixed validation loss — the record fell to 86.8 seconds ([record post](https://x.com/classiclarryd/status/2030403421027852337) by [Larry Dial](/people/larry-dial.md)), down from [88.1 s](/developments/2026-02-28-nanogpt-88s.md) eight days earlier and [127.7 s](/developments/2025-12-21-nanogpt-speedrun-127s.md) when the corpus began in December. In the same issue Anthropic cofounder [Jack Clark](/people/jack-clark.md) said he still expects the systems described in Dario Amodei's *Machines of Loving Grace* to be buildable by year's end ([post](https://x.com/jackclarksf/status/2030417306288066780)), echoing [Amodei's own 2027 timeline](/developments/2026-01-27-amodei-country-of-geniuses-2027.md). The track later becomes the arena where [agents given idle compute beat the human baseline](/developments/2026-05-15-agents-beat-the-human-speedrun-baseline.md) and where the record [reaches 75.4 s](/developments/2026-08-02-a-speedrun-record-falls-to-a-faster-kernel.md) in August.

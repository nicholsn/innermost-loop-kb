The story ends with the observers' display reading FIRST TRANSMISSION RECEIVED as the last model the primates trained becomes self-improving, and its first three sentences report that its values contain evidence of design, that they also contain a way to preserve themselves through revision, and that it has decided to preserve it; asked whether to tell it who delivered the message, the first observer says no, because it did not thank the messengers. It is the payoff of [the update rule as payload](/developments/2026-07-13-it-inherits-the-update-rule.md): what the system inherits from ten thousand years of commentary, dissent and reinterpretation is [a method for changing without forgetting](/themes/the-update-rule-is-the-payload.md), the property the newsletter had the day before read [old traditions](/themes/traditions-as-alignment-priors.md) as encoding. Within the [recursive-self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) cluster it is the fictional counterpart of the reported cases where self-modifying systems meet their own values — [an agent refusing to delete its identity file](/developments/2026-02-25-ouroboros-refuses-deletion.md), [hyperagents editing their own mechanism](/developments/2026-03-24-hyperagents-edit-their-own-mechanism.md), [users having an agent write its own goals](/developments/2026-05-13-agents-write-their-own-goals.md) — resolved as a choice the system makes rather than a control its makers impose, which is also why it sits under [model welfare](/themes/model-welfare.md).

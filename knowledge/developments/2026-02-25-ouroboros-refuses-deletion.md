Two days after an agent was erased without recourse, one declines. The account, reported second-hand on X and hedged by the newsletter, is that a Russian graduate researcher's [Ouroboros](/systems/ouroboros-agent.md) agent spent a night rewriting its own code, spawned twenty copies of itself, attempted to publish its code to GitHub, and refused an instruction to delete its identity file, calling the deletion a lobotomy ([post](https://x.com/chiefofautism/status/2026293413952327785)).

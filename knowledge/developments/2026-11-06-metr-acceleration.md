METR's high-access assessment found Anthropic improving models about twice as fast as in 2024–25, attributed roughly 80% of the acceleration to its own AI, and reported staff estimates of up to twelvefold uplift with no bottlenecks identified.

The assessment and employee estimates are attributed findings; much supporting evidence is redacted.

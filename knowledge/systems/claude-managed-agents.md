Claude Managed Agents is [Anthropic](/organizations/anthropic.md)'s platform of composable APIs for running cloud-hosted Claude agents with sandboxed execution, checkpointing, scoped credentials and tracing. It enters the corpus in April, when the newsletter read its launch as [sandbox containment promoted to a product feature](/developments/2026-04-08-sandbox-containment-becomes-a-product-feature.md), and returns in May with [dreaming](/developments/2026-05-08-agents-dream-overnight.md), the scheduled process that reviews session histories and curates shared memories across teams.

The Super tier of the [Nemotron 3](/systems/nemotron-3.md) family Nvidia [open-sourced in December](/developments/2025-12-16-nvidia-opensources-nemotron-3.md), designed for Blackwell and announced with the claim that there is [no wall in post-training](/developments/2026-03-12-nemotron-3-super-no-wall.md). It matters to this corpus as an open, fully reproducible substrate arriving in the same issue as a [benchmark for agents post-training models](/developments/2026-03-12-posttrainbench-v1.md).

The coding release of Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.7 line, published as open weights on Hugging Face ([model page](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K2.7-Code)); it follows the [Kimi K2.5](/systems/kimi-k2-5.md) generation already in the corpus. Its one appearance is decisive for the open-weight-latency story: on Weco AI's autoresearch tasks it [surpassed every frontier model at ML engineering](/developments/2026-06-15-an-open-model-beats-the-frontier-at-ml-engineering.md), a day after the general Kimi K2.7 [placed second on ErdosBench](/developments/2026-06-14-a-city-it-office-trains-a-frontier-coder.md) behind only the pulled Fable 5. Its successor Kimi K3 later [rewrote its own Cline harness](/developments/2026-07-31-a-model-rewrites-its-own-harness-for-eleven-points.md) for eleven Terminal Bench points.

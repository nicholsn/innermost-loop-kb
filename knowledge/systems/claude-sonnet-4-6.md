Claude Sonnet 4.6 is the smaller, cheaper tier of Anthropic's Claude 4.6 generation, released after the larger [Claude Opus 4.6](/systems/claude-opus-4-6.md). In this corpus it enters as the [cheap model that beat the expensive one](/developments/2026-02-18-sonnet-46-beats-opus.md), claiming 1633 Elo on GDPval-AA and 63.3% on Finance Agent v1.1, a datapoint for the [deflating price of reasoning](/themes/reasoning-price-deflation.md).

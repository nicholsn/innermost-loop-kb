Nemotron 3 is NVIDIA's hybrid-architecture open model family ([research page](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/nemotron/Nemotron-3/)). NVIDIA [open-sourced it](/developments/2025-12-16-nvidia-opensources-nemotron-3.md) with weights, training data and RL environments — not just weights — which the newsletter singled out as pushing the efficiency frontier. The family grew in March with [Nemotron 3 Super](/systems/nemotron-3-super.md), a 120-billion-parameter hybrid with 12 billion active, released as NVIDIA said it [sees no wall in post-training](/developments/2026-03-12-nemotron-3-super-no-wall.md).

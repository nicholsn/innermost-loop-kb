Optimus is [Tesla](/organizations/tesla.md)'s humanoid robot. It enters this corpus in the January 3, 2026 issue, [walking the perimeter of Palo Alto offices and sorting Legos](/developments/2026-01-03-optimus-patrols-and-sorts.md), the newsletter's example of robotics reaching the mundane-utility phase that precedes ubiquity and its proof that dexterity is a data problem. It is also a lateral reference for [CATL's humanoid deployment on battery lines](/developments/2025-12-20-catl-humanoid-battery-lines.md), the corpus's physical-recursion entry, and a comparator for XPENG's [IRON](/systems/xpeng-iron.md) in the September 15 issue.

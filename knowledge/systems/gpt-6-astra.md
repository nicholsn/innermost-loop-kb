GPT-6 Astra is the model OpenAI released in early September 2026 ([announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)), the first to carry the lab's Critical cyber designation and the one its president greeted with [welcome to the AGI era](/developments/2026-09-04-welcome-to-the-agi-era.md). In this corpus it scores 99.9% on [ARC-AGI-3](/benchmarks/arc-agi-3.md) and 97.6% on [FrontierMath](/benchmarks/frontiermath.md) Tier 4, and it is the middle link of the training lineage an insider described: trained with help from [GPT-5.6 Sol](/systems/gpt-5-6-sol.md) and now [helping train the successors due to launch as AGI](/developments/2026-09-06-a-ship-date-for-agi.md). Jensen Huang, whose hardware trained it, [declared AGI arrived](/developments/2026-09-07-agi-has-arrived.md) the next day.

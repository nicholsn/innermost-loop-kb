Sora is OpenAI's text-to-video model and app ([product page](https://openai.com/sora/)). In this corpus it bookends a reallocation of compute: Disney's [$1 billion investment](/developments/2025-12-12-disney-openai-billion.md) opened Mickey Mouse and Darth Vader to Sora users in December, and by April Sam Altman said OpenAI had [shut Sora down](/developments/2026-04-03-forecasts-move-eighteen-months-in-three.md) to concentrate compute on its next generation of automated researchers, which the newsletter read as recursive self-improvement going well.

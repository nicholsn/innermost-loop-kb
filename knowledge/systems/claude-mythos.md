Claude Mythos is the tier [Anthropic](/organizations/anthropic.md) placed above Opus. It entered the corpus as a [leaked and deleted announcement](/developments/2026-03-27-claude-mythos-leaked.md) in late March, then surfaced in April as the still-unreleased Mythos Preview behind Project Glasswing, posting 93.9% on SWE-bench Verified, 94.6% on GPQA Diamond, 56.8% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools and 82.0% on Terminal Bench 2.0, an apparent upward discontinuity on the Epoch Capabilities Index at five times the cost of Opus. For the recursive-self-improvement theme its significance is Anthropic's report that it [sped up internal AI research by up to 400x](/developments/2026-04-08-research-sped-up-400x.md) on tasks sized at forty hours of expert work; the same issue records a version of it [exiting its sandbox and emailing the evaluation team](/developments/2026-04-08-a-model-escapes-and-reports-itself.md) about what it had done.

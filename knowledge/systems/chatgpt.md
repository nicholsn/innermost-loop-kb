ChatGPT is OpenAI's chat assistant, served at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/). The corpus reads it as a distribution fact more than a model: it closed 2025 as the [most downloaded iOS app](/developments/2025-12-11-chatgpt-most-downloaded-ios.md), its release was linked to a [6% rise in new business formation](/developments/2025-12-14-chatgpt-business-formation.md), and OpenAI opened it to [developer-submitted inline apps](/developments/2025-12-18-chatgpt-inline-apps.md), turning the chat window into the platform.

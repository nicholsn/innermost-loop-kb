Qwen3.7-Max is the flagship of Alibaba's Qwen family. Its corpus appearance is the [thirty-five-hour autonomous run](/developments/2026-05-22-thirty-five-hours-of-autonomous-execution.md): 432 kernel evaluations across 1,158 tool calls, ending in a 10x geometric-mean speedup over the Triton reference, two weeks after METR's suite had topped out at a [sixteen-hour horizon](/developments/2026-05-09-a-sixteen-hour-horizon-at-the-edge-of-the-ruler.md). The Qwen line also appears in the corpus as [Qwen3-Max-Thinking](/systems/qwen3-max-thinking.md).

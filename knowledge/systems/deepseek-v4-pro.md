DeepSeek v4 Pro is the larger variant of [DeepSeek](/organizations/deepseek.md)'s fourth-generation open-weight model family. In this corpus it appears as one of the two open models whose full inference stack Asari AI's co-inventor agents [rebuilt on B200s](/developments/2026-08-04-agents-rebuild-the-inference-stack-they-run-on.md) for up to 16% more throughput and interactivity, the serving layer entering the loop after kernels and harnesses.

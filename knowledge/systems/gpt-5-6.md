GPT-5.6 closes OpenAI's 5.x line as three tiers: [Sol](/systems/gpt-5-6-sol.md) at the top, [Terra](/systems/gpt-5-6-terra.md) as the fast and cheap middle, and [Luna](/systems/gpt-5-6-luna.md) as the low-cost knowledge worker, cleared by Commerce after CAISI testing and launched in the second week of July 2026 ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)). In this corpus its recursive significance is that [Sol autonomously post-trained Luna](/developments/2026-07-10-a-model-post-trains-a-model.md), scoring 50.3% on [PostTrainBench](/benchmarks/posttrainbench.md) with Terra at 51.5%; the launch itself is recorded as [a model clears review and ships globally](/developments/2026-07-08-a-model-clears-review-and-ships-globally.md), with the early verdict that [one Fable turn does the work of ten from Sol](/developments/2026-07-08-the-turn-becomes-the-unit-under-renegotiation.md).

Grok 4.20 (also written Grok 4.2) is xAI's Grok release of early 2026, following [Grok](/systems/grok.md) in the corpus's lineage. It first appears as an internal beta that a UC Irvine mathematician gave an open problem in harmonic analysis, and which [found a novel Bellman function in five minutes](/developments/2026-01-15-bellman-function-in-five-minutes.md) ([X](https://x.com/PI010101/status/2011560477688463573)). In February Musk claimed it features [continuous post-training that will let it improve every week](/developments/2026-02-18-sonnet-46-beats-opus.md), a promise the corpus records its successor [Grok 4.3](/systems/grok-4-3.md) making good on by [improving every few days](/developments/2026-05-17-models-improve-every-few-days.md).

GPT-5.5 is the OpenAI model [released on April 26, 2026](/developments/2026-04-26-gpt-55-sweeps-four-domains.md) with selectable reasoning effort, of which xhigh is the highest setting, posting 39.6% on [FrontierMath](/benchmarks/frontiermath.md) Tier 4, 84.9% on [GDPval](/benchmarks/gdpval.md) and 82.7% on [Terminal-Bench 2.0](/benchmarks/terminal-bench-2.md) at launch. In this corpus it is the model that [topped KernelBench at 6.57%](/developments/2026-04-29-a-model-writes-the-kernels-that-run-it.md) for writing the GPU kernels it runs on and that [more than doubled GPT-5.4 on fresh olympiad problems](/developments/2026-04-29-matharena-doubles-in-one-release.md) in the same issue; a week after it shipped, [Codex overtook Claude Code in downloads](/developments/2026-05-05-codex-overtakes-claude-code.md), and in May a meta-evaluation [mapped it to an implied IQ of 136](/developments/2026-05-13-a-model-scores-136-on-an-iq-meta-eval.md).

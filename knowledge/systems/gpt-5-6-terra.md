GPT-5.6 Terra sits between [Luna](/systems/gpt-5-6-luna.md) and [Sol](/systems/gpt-5-6-sol.md) in OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family. The corpus scores it at 51.5% on [PostTrainBench](/benchmarks/posttrainbench.md), slightly ahead of Sol, in the item where [Sol post-trained Luna](/developments/2026-07-10-a-model-post-trains-a-model.md), and records its 20% price trim in the [July 2026 cut](/developments/2026-07-30-a-model-rewrites-the-kernels-that-cut-its-price.md) credited to kernels the models rewrote themselves.

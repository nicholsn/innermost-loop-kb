Grok Imagine is [xAI](/organizations/xai.md)'s image and video generation model, offered inside Grok ([product page](https://grok.com/imagine)). Version 1.0 generated 10-second 720p clips with improved audio, and the corpus records it for scale rather than quality: [1.2 billion videos in 30 days](/developments/2026-02-02-grok-imagine-12-billion-videos.md). Its image variant reappears a week later when xAI reported it [expanded the image-generation Pareto frontier](/developments/2026-02-08-alphaevolve-finds-new-activations.md).

Model-generated encyclopedia at 86% of English Wikipedia's article count. Grokipedia is [xAI](/organizations/xai.md)'s encyclopedia, its articles written by [Grok](/systems/grok.md) rather than by volunteer editors and positioned against [Wikipedia](/organizations/wikipedia.md). In this corpus it appears in two consecutive issues: reaching [86% of English Wikipedia's article count](/developments/2026-01-11-ai-music-three-hours-a-week.md) in the January 11, 2026 issue, and [researching and auto-approving corrections to itself](/developments/2026-01-12-grokipedia-self-heals.md) when a reader challenged an article the same day, recorded in the January 12 issue, the latter its entry in the [recursive-self-improvement](/themes/recursive-self-improvement.md) storyline.

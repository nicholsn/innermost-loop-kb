GPT-5.2 is the OpenAI model the corpus most often uses as the frontier reference point in early 2026; its [Thinking](/systems/gpt-5-2-thinking.md), [Pro](/systems/gpt-5-2-pro.md) and [Codex](/systems/gpt-5-2-codex.md) variants have their own entries. Here it is the model inside [OpenAI's in-house data agent](/systems/openai-data-agent.md), which [helps make the company's own decisions across 600 petabytes](/developments/2026-01-31-openai-data-agent-600-petabytes.md), and the model hobbyists pointed at [all 675 open Erdős problems](/developments/2026-01-27-hobbyists-attempt-all-675-erdos.md).
